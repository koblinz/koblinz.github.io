FAQ

How do I play? Drag anywhere on the screen to move Terri. He attacks on his own. Collect the gems enemies drop to level up, then pick a new weapon or upgrade. Survive three waves and beat the boss to clear a level.

How do weapons evolve? Raise a weapon to its maximum level while you own its matching item. The level-up card tells you which item each weapon needs.

Can I start from a later level? Yes. Once you reach a new land, tap its glowing medal on the home screen to start your next run there.

I can't hear any sound. Check the sound button at the top right of the home screen, and your iPhone's volume.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, open the pause menu during a run and tap "Ad privacy choices". Anywhere, you can turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
