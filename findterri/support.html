Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Email koblin1@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

How do I play?
Each page hides Terri, some of his friends, his lost gear, a story treasure and a guest. Pinch or double-tap to zoom, drag to look around, and tap a character when you spot them. The faster you find everyone, the more stars and coins you earn.

I'm stuck!
If you go a while without finding anyone, a magnifier clue button appears. Tap it to see roughly where your next target is hiding.

How do I unlock new pages?
Finish a page, then spend coins to open the next one. Coins come from finds, coins and treasure chests hidden on each page, the speed bonus, the daily gift and the Lucky Spin.

Can I watch the story again?
Yes. Tap STORY on the title screen to replay every chapter you've unlocked.

I can't hear any sound.
Check the sound button on the title screen and the Music and Sound switches in the pause menu, and turn your device's volume up.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Your progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app deletes it.

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Find Terri plays offline. When you're online it shows ads, and if you're signed in to Game Center your VS Mode scores go to the world leaderboard.

I bought something. How do I get it on a new phone?
Open the SHOP and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Remove Ads, the Golden Ticket, the All Pages Pass, the Starter Pack and Chapter 2: The Mirror Kingdom come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Coins and clues are used up as you play, so they can't be restored.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

What is Chapter 2: The Mirror Kingdom?
Ten extra, harder pages (31 to 40) with a brand-new story, new characters and glass Terri look-alikes. Unlock it in the SHOP or from the map. It's included in the Golden Ticket.

Why are there ads?
Ads keep Find Terri free. You'll see a small banner on the menus, an occasional ad between pages, and videos you choose to watch for a reward. Never in the middle of a page. Remove Ads (or the Starter Pack or Golden Ticket) turns off the banner and the ads between pages.
