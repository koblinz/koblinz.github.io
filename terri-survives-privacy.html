Terri Survives — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Terri Survives ("the game") is made by an independent developer (the seller shown on its App Store page). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

Terri Survives is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. The game itself keeps your progress only on your device. The ads are the only part of the game that uses the internet, and they are explained below.

What is stored, and where

Your progress (gold, gems, levels cleared, armor, pets, relics, quests and settings) is saved only on your device, inside the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional "watch an ad" rewards (for example to revive Terri, double your gold, reroll upgrade choices or spin the Lucky Wheel again) and an occasional full-screen ad between runs. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the app asks; you can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking),
  • general device and app information (such as device model, operating system, approximate location from your IP address) and
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps).

If you don't allow tracking, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time from the pause menu ("Ad privacy choices"). Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy for partner apps.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • We don't collect, see or sell any of your personal information ourselves.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No real-money purchases inside the game.

Children

Terri Survives is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
