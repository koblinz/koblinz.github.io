FAQ

How do I play?
Swipe through attackers before they reach Terri. Slash glowing hearts to heal and avoid the skulls. When the meter at the bottom is full, tap it to unleash Lash Out.

I can't hear any sound.
Check the sound button on the title screen and the Music and Sound FX switches in the pause menu, and turn your phone's volume up.

Can I turn off vibration or flashing effects?
Yes. Open the pause menu during a game and switch off Haptics or Flashes.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Your progress is saved only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it.

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Save Terri plays offline. When you're online, it shows ads, and if you're signed in to Game Center your best scores also go to the world leaderboard (title screen → SCORES → WORLD RANKINGS).

Why are there ads?
Ads keep Save Terri free. You'll only see a small banner on the menus, an occasional ad between runs, and videos you choose to watch for a reward. Never in the middle of a run.
